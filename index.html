VIS live crowd

A phone on the balcony watches the room. Roboflow finds and tracks each person, and the display draws the seat map filling up. Only positions leave the phone; no video is recorded or shown.

CameraOpen on the phone on the balcony. DisplayOpen on the laptop going to AVT, then fullscreen.
  1. Put the phone and the laptop on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Display on the laptop and press F for fullscreen.
  3. Open Camera on the phone, enter the Roboflow API key once, then tap Calibrate and drag the four points onto the outer chairs.
  4. Tap Start. The phone shows people and fps; the display switches from simulated to live within a few seconds.
  5. If live positions stop, the display keeps going on its own. Press S on the laptop to see status.
